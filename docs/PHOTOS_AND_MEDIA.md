# Accra Spaces — Photos & Media Policy

## Purpose

Day and night photos exist to show lighting, street visibility and street presence honestly — especially for shops and street-front spaces. They are a trust feature, not decoration.

## Rules

- **Day and night as a pair:** a listing only earns the *completeness* cue when it has at least one day photo and one night photo.
- **Private by default:** the S3 bucket is private; photos are served through short-lived signed URLs generated by the API. There is no public bucket and no predictable public URL.
- **Constrained uploads:** presigned POST limited to JPEG, PNG and WebP, maximum 5 MB, expiry 5 minutes, key bound to the poster's listing.
- **EXIF stripped server-side** on upload (location and device metadata are not retained).
- **Watermarking** (planned, v2): server-side watermark via Lambda + sharp so images can't be lifted easily. Not shipped until it works — no claims of protection before then.
- **Retention:** photos live while the listing is active; on disable/removal they are retained for a grace window (default 90 days) then expired by lifecycle policy. `abort-incomplete-multipart-upload` after 7 days.

## Bucket layout

```text
listings/{listing_id}/day/{uuid}.jpg
listings/{listing_id}/night/{uuid}.jpg
pending/{...}      → 30-day expiry (abandoned uploads)
```

## Safety

- No photos are made public before the listing is published.
- Reportable content: inappropriate, misleading or stolen photos can be reported per listing; moderation can disable the listing (v1) — the full moderation dashboard is v2.
